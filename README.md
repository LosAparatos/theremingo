# Theremingo

A handmade analog electromagnetic theremin, designed and built by [Los Aparatos](https://losaparatos.com.ar) in Tucumán, Argentina.

## What is it?

Theremingo is an analog theremin — a touchless electronic instrument played by moving your hands near its antennas, which affect pitch and volume through electromagnetic field disturbance. No microcontroller, no code: fully analog circuitry.

## About Los Aparatos

Los Aparatos is a one-person workshop building experimental electronic instruments — synthesizers, sound toys and DIY kits — by hand in Tucumán, Argentina, since 2007.

Other instruments: [Auduina](https://gitlab.com/los-aparatos/auduina) (granular synth), Drawdio (a sound pencil that turns drawing into sound), CoyuyoC (a drone synth).
